# Competitors: public summary

The full teardown (per-site technical checks, off-site findings, outreach plans) is kept private,
because this repository is public. This file keeps what the build and content work need. Research
date: 2026-10-24.

## Sites worth learning from

| Site | Type | What it does well |
|---|---|---|
| Mare Vasile (`marevasile.ro`) | Reghin family workshop | Numbered instrument pages named after the historical model ("Vioară de maestru №303, Stradivari Joachim 1715"), "lutier Reghin" in the title, ro/en/de with hreflang, LocalBusiness markup with coordinates, a press post |
| Gliga (`gligaviolins.com`) | Reghin factory | 15 languages with hreflang + x-default. Product pages carry FAQ, Product/Offer and shipping/returns markup, and short direct answers that AI tools can quote |
| Corilon (Munich) | Dealer | Multilingual product pages with an audio clip per instrument |
| MyLuthier (London) | Dealer | New maker-built instruments sold on the maker's name, with price, model, year and maker on the first screen, plus video |
| Hora (`hora.ro`) | Reghin factory | Helper guides for sizes, tuning and models |

## Patterns among the strongest sites

1. **One page per physical instrument**, plus category pages by instrument (often by size and level),
   a maker page, a workshop/process page, a press or blog section, and an archive of sold work.
2. **Descriptive titles**: model or historical original + instrument + maker + year (+ place). No
   strong site uses generic titles like "Cello 4/4".
3. **Readable slugs** containing the model and a number or maker. No brackets or counters.
4. **Plain schema**: Organization/LocalBusiness with address and geo everywhere. The leaders add
   Product + Offer, Person for the maker, FAQ, and shipping/returns.
5. **Audio or video on the product page** is rare among Reghin independents and common among the
   best dealers.
6. **Prices**: independent makers in Reghin and the EU almost never publish them. Dealers and
   factories do. Our public EUR prices are a point of difference, and they give AI tools a figure to
   quote.
7. **Reghin as a selling point**: the independents who rank write "lutier Reghin" in titles and use
   the "City of Violins" line, which positions them as individual makers rather than a factory.

## Neutral sources about Reghin

Wikipedia (Reghin, Hora, Vasile Gliga, Roman Boianciuc), Visit Mureș (county tourism), Homo Faber
(European craft directory), Wikidata, Radio Romania International, and Romanian national press
(Agerpres, Digi24, ProTV, Observator, Newsweek România). Today they name the factories and a few
workshops. Being named in such sources is the main off-site goal.
